01 · CODE-REVIEWED, NOT A LIVE-SERVICE CLAIM

Verified ValhallaOS capabilities

  • The public OS desk can connect to display a wallet's public address; connection alone does not load balances or positions.
  • Paper trades are local simulations with assumed inputs. They do not request a signature, create an order, or execute a transaction.
  • Live trading, rewards payouts, and hive actions are disabled in the inspected public configuration.
  • The account desk checks sessions with its configured auth service. Provider availability and production behavior remain unverified.

These are code and fixture findings, not a claim that external services are live or that the software is audited.
