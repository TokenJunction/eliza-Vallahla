Product order

The website at /app is the main product. You sign in, see your own wallet, and use the desk in the browser. The Chrome extension is the second way in. Telegram is coming soon. The Android app is in testing and is not a public download yet. Its app id is ai.tokenjunction.valhalla. Windows is paused and coming soon.

What the desk does

The desk can show public balances and a paper lab. Keys stay on your device. The website does not keep a seed phrase. It does not send or withdraw. Sample panels are labeled DEMO DATA.

Hive

The Hive is optional volunteer compute for Forge Lab paper tests. It is off until a leader turns it on, and it stays off for you until you choose it. The Chrome extension has a separate idle-helper switch, also off by default. A job is public prices and public strategy settings. Two or more browsers must return the same paper result before it counts. The work stops when you stop it, when the tab or Chrome closes, or when the computer is busy, on battery, or on a metered connection. It does not mine. It does not share your connection.

Points

Verified paper work can add contribution points to an account. Points are not a coin, not cash, and not transferable. Rewards are points only for now. A later payout design exists in the code and is switched off. It would need a public payout address, a separate rewards treasury, a minimum point count, and leader approval. This paper does not state an amount or a rate.
