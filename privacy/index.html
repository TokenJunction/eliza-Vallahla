PRIVACY

Valhalla privacy policy

TokenJunction LLC publishes Valhalla. TokenJunction LLC is a private company. It is the owner of Valhalla.

Valhalla is a companion for a desk that runs on your computer. The phone and the Chrome extension do not hold a wallet key. They cannot send, withdraw, or take custody of funds.

Chrome extension

The extension stores, on this browser only, the desk address you type and a pairing token for the desk on this computer. It does not collect email, contacts, location, or web history. It does not send that data to TokenJunction. You can remove it from chrome://extensions.

Data on the phone

The phone can store, on that phone only, the desk address, an optional access code, the last status, and chat or error lines. A Phantom or Solflare connect shares the public key only. The app does not store a wallet seed or a secret key. There is no Valhalla account on a server.

Ads

The free plan can show ads. Pro does not. Ads are not shown on wallet connect or settings, and a full-screen ad does not run when the app opens. Google AdMob may use the advertising identifier. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

This website

A request form opens a message to hello@valhalla-app.xyz. It does not ask for a wallet key or a seed phrase.

Contact

Support is hello@valhalla-app.xyz. The site is https://valhalla-app.xyz. This page does not include a street address or a tax id.
