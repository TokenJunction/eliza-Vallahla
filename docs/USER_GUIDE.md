# ValOS public desk

This is the public site at https://valhalla-app.xyz.

## Sign in

Open the site. Choose **Continue with Google**, or enter an email address and choose **Email me a sign-in link**. The link returns you to the site signed in.

## The desk

After sign-in, the desk is at https://valhalla-app.xyz/os/. Live trading is off. The desk does not ask the wallet to sign or send a transaction.

## The assistant

The assistant panel on `/os/` sends the question to `/api/ai` with the signed-in session. The page does not ask for an AI key and does not store one in the browser. A missing server configuration shows "Assistant not connected".
