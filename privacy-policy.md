# Valhalla privacy policy

TokenJunction LLC publishes Valhalla. TokenJunction LLC is a private company. It is the parent company and the owner of Valhalla.

Valhalla is a phone companion for a desk that runs on your computer. The phone does not hold a wallet key. It cannot send, withdraw, or take custody of funds.

## Data on the phone

The phone can store, on that phone only:

- The desk address and an optional access code
- The last status, chat lines, and error lines
- Counts of how many times a house message was shown or opened

It does not store a wallet seed, a secret key, or a payment card. Delete phone data is on the error screen. There is no Valhalla account on a server.

## Ads

If ads are on, Google AdMob may use the advertising identifier. See https://policies.google.com/privacy. The app asks for consent where GDPR or CCPA requires it. House-message counts stay on the phone.

## This website

The waitlist and API request open a message to the project. They do not ask for a wallet key or a seed phrase. If a form service is connected later, only the email and the note you type are sent.

## The desk

The phone sends chat text and confirmed setting changes to the desk address you enter. The Google AI Studio key stays on the computer.
